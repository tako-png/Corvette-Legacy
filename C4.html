Fourth generation (C4; 1983–1996)

The Corvette was completely reengineered for 1984 (there was no 1983 model year), introducing a sleeker, more aerodynamic body and a fully digital dashboard. This generation focused heavily on modern handling and technology, closing out with the high-performance ZR-1 "King of the Hill."


  • Design: Smooth, wedge-shaped body with a large rear glass hatch (from 1984)
  • Chassis: All-new uniframe construction for improved rigidity
  • Digital dashboard: Introduced electronic instrument cluster with digital speedometer
  • ZR-1 (1990–1995): High-performance model with a DOHC LT5 V8 producing up to 405 hp, developed with Lotus
  • Convertible returns: Reintroduced in 1986 after a 10-year absence
  • Engine options: Small-block V8s ranging from 205 hp (early years) up to 300 hp (LT1, 1992+)
  • Anti-lock brakes & traction control: Added mid-generation as standard safety tech
  • 40th Anniversary Edition: Special Ruby Red package in 1993
  • Production numbers: Roughly 358,000 units built over the generation
