Fifth generation (C5; 1997–2004)

The C5 brought a ground-up redesign with a new hydroformed frame, dramatically improving structural rigidity and handling. It's often credited with restoring the Corvette's reputation as a world-class sports car after a rough patch in the late C4 years.

  • Chassis: New hydroformed steel frame, much stiffer than previous generations
  • Rear-mounted transmission (transaxle): Improved weight distribution front-to-rear
  • Engine: All-new LS1 small-block V8, 345 hp, lighter and more efficient
  • Fixed-roof Coupe (FRC): Lightweight hardtop-only model introduced in 1999
  • Z06 (2001–2004): High-performance variant with up to 405 hp (LS6 engine)
  • Design: Smoother, more rounded styling compared to the C4
  • Interior: Significant quality and comfort improvements over prior generation
  • Top speed: Up to 172 mph in Z06 trim
  • Production numbers: Around 248,000 units built over the generation
