Sixth generation (C6; 2005–2013)


The C6 refined the C5's formula with exposed headlights (a first since 1962) and a tighter, more focused design. It brought the Corvette's performance to new heights with the introduction of the supercharged ZR1.



  • Design: Fixed headlights (replacing pop-ups) for the first time since 1962
  • Engine: LS2, later LS3 small-block V8s, up to 436 hp in base Corvettes
  • Z06 (2006+): 427 cu in LS7 V8, 505 hp, aluminum frame for weight savings
  • ZR1 (2009–2013): Supercharged LS9 V8 producing 638 hp — most powerful Corvette at the time
  • Top speed: Up to 205 mph in ZR1 trim
  • Interior: Improved materials and technology, including navigation and upgraded audio
  • Grand Sport trim: Introduced in 2010, combining Z06 wide-body styling with base V8 performance
  • Production numbers: Around 215,000 units built over the generation
