Seventh generation (C7; 2014–2019)

The C7 marked a major styling overhaul with sharper, more aggressive lines and the return of the "Stingray" name. This generation pushed the Corvette firmly into supercar territory with the introduction of the mid-engine-adjacent ZR1's massive power output.

  • Name revival: "Stingray" officially returns as the base trim name
  • Design: Angular, aggressive styling with quad taillights and a wide stance
  • Engine: LT1 small-block V8, 460 hp (base Stingray)
  • Z06 (2015+): Supercharged LT4 V8, 650 hp
  • ZR1 (2019): Supercharged LT5 V8, 755 hp — most powerful Corvette ever at release
  • Transmission: 7-speed manual or 8-speed automatic (paddle-shift)
  • Interior: Major quality upgrade, customizable digital gauge cluster
  • Top speed: Up to 212 mph in ZR1 trim
  • Production numbers: Roughly 168,000 units built over the generation
