Eighth generation (C8; 2020–present)

The C8 marks the most radical shift in Corvette history: the engine moved from the front to the middle of the car for the first time, transforming the Corvette into a true mid-engine sports car and putting it in direct competition with exotic European supercars.


  • Layout change: First-ever mid-engine Corvette (engine behind the driver)
  • Design: Dramatically reshaped, low, wide body with supercar-style proportions
  • Engine: LT2 small-block V8, 495 hp (with performance exhaust)
  • Transmission: 8-speed dual-clutch automatic only (no manual offered)
  • Z06 (2023+): High-revving naturally aspirated flat-plane-crank V8, 670 hp
  • E-Ray (2024+): First hybrid/all-wheel-drive Corvette, combining V8 power with an electric motor
  • Removable roof panel: Convertible and coupe versions both available
  • Interior: Driver-focused cockpit with vertical button "wall" and digital displays
  • Production status: Currently in production, ongoing sales
